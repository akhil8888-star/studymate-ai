# StudyMate AI 🚀

StudyMate AI is an AI-powered learning companion that helps students learn smarter by transforming study materials into interactive learning experiences.

## 🌟 Features

* 📄 Upload notes, PDFs, and study materials
* 🧠 Automatic concept extraction and analysis
* 🌳 AI-generated mind maps for every study session
* ❓ Smart quiz generation
* 🃏 Automatic flashcard creation
* 📅 Personalized revision schedules
* 🔥 Study streak tracking
* 📊 Learning analytics and progress insights
* 🎓 AI tutor for personalized explanations

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often struggle with:

* Forgetting what they study
* Managing multiple learning resources
* Identifying weak topics
* Maintaining study consistency
* Planning effective revisions
* Tracking learning progress

## 💡 Solution

StudyMate AI transforms study materials into interactive learning resources such as mind maps, quizzes, flashcards, and revision plans while helping students stay consistent and improve retention.
